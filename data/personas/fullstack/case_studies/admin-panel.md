# Internal admin panel

## Context
The brief from this fintech company was simple to say and hard to do: an internal tool so our support staff can look up and fix customer accounts. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using React, NestJS, Docker. Under the hood it relies on a relational database with strong constraints. Under the hood it relies on delegated sign-in with access and refresh tokens. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system support handled tickets 35% faster with one screen instead of five tools.
