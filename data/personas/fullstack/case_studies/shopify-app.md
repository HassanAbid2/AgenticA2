# Custom Shopify app

## Context
A e-commerce client came to me needing a custom app for our store that offers product bundles at checkout. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using GraphQL. Under the hood it relies on a hosted e-commerce platform and its app APIs. Under the hood it relies on server-side JavaScript. Under the hood it relies on component-based UI with hooks. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system average order value up 11% across 60 stores.
