# Real-time logistics tracker

## Context
The brief from this logistics company was simple to say and hard to do: a live map showing where every delivery van is right now. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using React, Node.js, WebSockets, PostgreSQL, Docker. Under the hood it relies on an in-memory store used for caching and pub/sub. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 1,200 trucks on a live map with sub-second updates.
