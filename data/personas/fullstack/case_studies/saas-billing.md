# SaaS subscription dashboard

## Context
A SaaS client came to me needing a customer portal where users manage plans, invoices and payment methods. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using TypeScript, Next.js, Stripe, PostgreSQL. Under the hood it relies on component-based UI with hooks. Under the hood it relies on a type-safe ORM generated from a schema file. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system failed-payment recovery up 22%; churned MRR down $9k a month.
