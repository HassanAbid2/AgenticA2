# Event ticketing PWA

## Context
The brief from this events company was simple to say and hard to do: sell tickets for our events and scan them at the door from a phone. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Firebase, Tailwind CSS. Under the hood it relies on component-based UI with hooks. Under the hood it relies on card payments, subscriptions and billing webhooks. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system sold 26k tickets; QR check-in 3 seconds per guest.
