# Classroom chat with presence

## Context
A education client came to me needing real-time chat between teachers and students with online indicators. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Express, Redis, React. Under the hood it relies on server-side JavaScript. Under the hood it relies on live two-way updates pushed to the browser. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 8,000 concurrent students on two small instances.

- Express
- Redis
