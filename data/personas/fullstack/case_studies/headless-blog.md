# Headless CMS blog

## Context
A media client came to me needing rebuild our blog so editors can publish without a developer. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Next.js, GraphQL, TypeScript, Vercel.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system organic traffic up 40% after the rebuild.
