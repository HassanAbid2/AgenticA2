# Landing page performance overhaul

## Context
The brief from this marketing company was simple to say and hard to do: make our marketing site load fast and look modern. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Next.js, Tailwind CSS, React. Under the hood it relies on edge hosting with preview deployments. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system Lighthouse score 54 to 98; paid-ad conversion up 9%.
