# Mobile fitness app

## Context
This engagement (health & fitness) started with a pain point - an iOS and Android app for logging workouts and tracking progress. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Firebase, TypeScript. Under the hood it relies on one codebase for iOS and Android. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 4.7-star rating, 18k installs in 3 months.

## Stack
- Firebase
- TypeScript
