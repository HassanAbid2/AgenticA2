# Clinic appointment booking

## Context
This engagement (healthcare) started with a pain point - online booking for our clinics with SMS reminders. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Celery, Docker. Under the hood it relies on a batteries-included Python web framework. Under the hood it relies on a relational database with strong constraints. Under the hood it relies on component-based UI with hooks. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system no-shows down 28% thanks to automated reminders.
