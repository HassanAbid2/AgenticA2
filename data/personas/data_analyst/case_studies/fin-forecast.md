# Financial forecasting model

## Context
A fintech client came to me needing a cash-flow forecast our CFO can update each week. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Excel, forecasting, Python, Google Sheets.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 13-week cash forecast within 4% of actuals.
