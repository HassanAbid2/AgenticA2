# SaaS churn diagnostic

## Context
The brief from this SaaS company was simple to say and hard to do: figure out which customers are likely to cancel and why. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using SQL, Metabase, cohort analysis, Python.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system pinpointed two features linked to churn; save-desk targeting improved 20%.
