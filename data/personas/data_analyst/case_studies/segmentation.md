# Customer segmentation

## Context
This engagement (e-commerce) started with a pain point - group our customers so marketing can target them differently. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, pandas, SQL. Under the hood it relies on grouping similar customers without labels. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 5 segments drove a targeted email that lifted revenue per send 23%.
