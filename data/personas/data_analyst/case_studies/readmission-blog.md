# Lessons from building CareReturn

*Posted 2023-09*

Last year I spent a few months on CareReturn, a project for a healthcare team. The ask: analyse why patients come back within 30 days of discharge. Honestly the hardest part wasn't the model or the code, it was getting clean inputs. We ended up using SQL and Tableau, plus tidyverse analysis and ggplot charts. End result: it identified 3 discharge factors; readmissions down 9% after the pilot.

What I'd do differently: start with evaluation earlier.
