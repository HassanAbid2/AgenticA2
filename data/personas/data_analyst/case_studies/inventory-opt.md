# Inventory optimisation analysis

## Context
A retail client came to me needing decide how much stock to hold per product without running out. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, pandas, forecasting, Excel.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system safety stock cut 15% with no rise in stock-outs.
