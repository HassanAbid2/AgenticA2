# Hospital readmission analysis

## Context
The brief from this healthcare company was simple to say and hard to do: analyse why patients come back within 30 days of discharge. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using statistics, SQL, Tableau. Under the hood it relies on tidyverse analysis and ggplot charts. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system identified 3 discharge factors; readmissions down 9% after the pilot.
