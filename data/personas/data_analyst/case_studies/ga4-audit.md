# GA4 migration and tracking audit

## Context
The brief from this media company was simple to say and hard to do: fix our website analytics; the numbers don't match our sales. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Google Sheets, Looker Studio. Under the hood it relies on web and app event tracking (GA4). I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system fixed 31 broken events; conversion tracking matches the backend within 2%.

- Google Sheets
- Looker Studio
