# Cohort retention analysis

## Context
A subscription apps client came to me needing understand when and why our subscribers leave. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using SQL, BigQuery, Looker. Under the hood it relies on tracking groups of users by signup month. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system found the week-2 drop-off; onboarding fix lifted 90-day retention 6 points.

- SQL
