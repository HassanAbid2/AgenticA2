# Excel automation for finance

## Context
This engagement (accounting) started with a pain point - automate the spreadsheets our finance team rebuilds every month. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Excel, VBA, Power Query.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system month-end reporting from 3 days to 4 hours.
