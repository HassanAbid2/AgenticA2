# Pricing elasticity study

## Context
The brief from this retail company was simple to say and hard to do: find out how sensitive our customers are to price changes. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using R, statistics. Under the hood it relies on controlled experiments with holdout groups. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system price changes on 120 SKUs added 3.1% gross margin.
