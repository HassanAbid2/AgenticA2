# Executive Tableau suite

## Context
The brief from this real estate company was simple to say and hard to do: executive dashboards for occupancy, rent roll and pipeline. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Tableau, SQL, dashboards. Under the hood it relies on a cloud data warehouse with separate compute and storage. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system adopted by 35 executives; 4 legacy reports retired.
