# Lessons from building CloseFast

*Posted 2022-09*

Last year I spent a few months on CloseFast, a project for a accounting team. The ask: automate the spreadsheets our finance team rebuilds every month. Honestly the hardest part wasn't the model or the code, it was getting clean inputs. We ended up using Excel and VBA, plus repeatable spreadsheet data transforms. End result: it month-end reporting from 3 days to 4 hours.

What I'd do differently: start with evaluation earlier.
