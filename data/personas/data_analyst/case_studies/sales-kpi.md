# Sales KPI dashboard

## Context
A retail client came to me needing one dashboard for our sales numbers instead of a dozen spreadsheets. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using SQL, Excel. Under the hood it relies on Microsoft's BI suite with DAX measures. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system replaced 14 weekly spreadsheets; leadership meeting prep down 6 hours.
