# Operations dashboards in Metabase

## Context
The brief from this logistics company was simple to say and hard to do: simple self-serve dashboards for our warehouse managers. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Metabase, SQL, dashboards. Under the hood it relies on a relational database with strong constraints. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system warehouse leads check 12 live KPIs instead of asking analysts.
