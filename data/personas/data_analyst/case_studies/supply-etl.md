# Supply-chain ETL pipeline

## Context
The brief from this logistics company was simple to say and hard to do: get shipment data from our carriers into one place automatically. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Airflow, Python, ETL, Redshift. Under the hood it relies on a relational database with strong constraints. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system data freshness from 24 hours to 1 hour.
