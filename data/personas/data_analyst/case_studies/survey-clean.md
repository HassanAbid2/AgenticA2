# Survey data cleaning and insights

## Context
A nonprofit client came to me needing tidy up messy survey responses and summarise what people said. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, pandas, data cleaning, Excel.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system cleaned 22k messy responses; board report delivered in 5 days.
