# Lessons from building SurveySense

*Posted 2022-04*

Last year I spent a few months on SurveySense, a project for a nonprofit team. The ask: tidy up messy survey responses and summarise what people said. Honestly the hardest part wasn't the model or the code, it was getting clean inputs. We ended up using Python and pandas and Excel, plus fixing messy, duplicated and inconsistent records. End result: it cleaned 22k messy responses; board report delivered in 5 days.

What I'd do differently: start with evaluation earlier.
