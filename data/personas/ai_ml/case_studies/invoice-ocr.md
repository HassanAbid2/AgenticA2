# Invoice OCR pipeline

## Context
The brief from this fintech company was simple to say and hard to do: read supplier invoices (PDF scans) and fill our accounting system automatically. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, computer vision, OpenCV, Celery, FastAPI, AWS.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system 98.2% field-level accuracy at 20,000 documents a day.
