# Retail demand forecasting

## Context
The brief from this retail company was simple to say and hard to do: forecast weekly demand per product and store so we stop over-ordering. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, time series forecasting, LightGBM, pandas, Airflow.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system weekly MAPE fell from 24% to 13% across 1,800 SKUs.
