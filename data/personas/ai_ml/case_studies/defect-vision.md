# Assembly-line defect detection

## Context
This engagement (manufacturing) started with a pain point - a camera system that spots scratched or misaligned parts on our production line. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, computer vision, YOLO, Docker. Under the hood it relies on a dynamic-graph deep learning framework from Meta. Under the hood it relies on the classic image-processing library. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system mAP 0.88 at 30 FPS on an edge device; scrap rate down 17%.
