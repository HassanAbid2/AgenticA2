# Shopping assistant chatbot

## Context
The brief from this e-commerce company was simple to say and hard to do: a chatbot on our store that answers product questions from our catalogue. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, LangChain, RAG, OpenAI API, FastAPI.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system handled 62% of pre-sales questions without a human.
