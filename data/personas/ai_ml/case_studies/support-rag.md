# Support-ticket RAG assistant

## Context
This engagement (SaaS) started with a pain point - an assistant that answers customer questions from our help-centre articles. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, LangChain, FastAPI, OpenAI API, Docker. Under the hood it relies on answers grounded in documents retrieved at question time. Under the hood it relies on a self-hosted vector database written in Rust. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system cut median first-response time by 38% across 4,100 tickets a month.
