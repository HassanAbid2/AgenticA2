# Insurance pricing model

## Context
A insurance client came to me needing improve our car-insurance premium model with modern ML. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, pandas, scikit-learn. Under the hood it relies on gradient-boosted decision trees. I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system Gini up 0.06 over the GLM in production.
