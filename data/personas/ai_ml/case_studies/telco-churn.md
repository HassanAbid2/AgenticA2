# Telecom churn model

## Context
A telecom client came to me needing predict which subscribers are about to cancel so our retention team can call them. They had tried an off-the-shelf tool first and it did not fit their workflow.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Python, XGBoost, scikit-learn, pandas, MLflow.  I wrote tests for the critical paths and documented the handover.

## Results
The system recall 0.82 at 0.6 precision; retention team estimated $140k a year saved.
